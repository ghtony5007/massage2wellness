Post-Session Care Guide

Follow these simple steps after each appointment to get the most out of your massage.

Drink Water

Drink at least 2–3 glasses of water in the hours following your session to help your body flush metabolic waste released during massage.

Rest

Avoid intense workouts for at least 12 hours after a deep tissue session. Light walking is fine. Let your muscles integrate the work.

Apply Heat if Sore

Some soreness 24–48 hours after deep tissue work is normal. A warm bath or heating pad can ease any temporary discomfort.

Avoid Alcohol

Skip alcohol for the rest of the day after your appointment. Massage increases circulation and alcohol can amplify its effects unexpectedly.

Book Regularly

For stress management, aim for once a month. For chronic pain or athletic recovery, every 2–3 weeks delivers noticeably better results.

Give Feedback

Let your therapist know what worked and what didn’t. A brief note at your next session helps us tailor pressure and focus areas perfectly.
